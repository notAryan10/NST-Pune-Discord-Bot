# NST Admissions

## Overview
Newton School of Technology (NST) offers a 4-year residential undergraduate B.Tech program in Computer Science & AI through partner universities. The program is designed around computer science foundations, AI/ML, data science, hands-on projects, mentorship, and industry exposure.

NST currently operates programs with partner universities including Rishihood University, Ajeenkya DY Patil University (Pune), S-VYASA University (Bengaluru), and St. Mary's University (Hyderabad). The degree is awarded by the relevant partner university.

## Admission route
The standard admission journey is:

1. Register for the Newton Scholastic Aptitude Test (NSAT), when applicable.
2. Take the online NSAT.
3. Complete the interview round if shortlisted.
4. Attend the applicable counselling process.
5. Complete admission/document verification.
6. Block the seat and complete the required fee formalities.
7. Join the residential campus and hostel.

Admission requirements, dates, test structure, and procedures can change between admission cycles.

## NSAT
NSAT is Newton School of Technology's entrance assessment. The official NST admission material describes assessment areas including mathematics, English, and general aptitude/logical reasoning. The exact syllabus, dates, eligibility, and process should always be checked against the current official admission information.

## Profile-based admission
NST may also shortlist strong candidates based on profiles such as:
- JEE performance
- Academic excellence
- Coding/competitive-programming profiles
- Technical achievements

A profile-based shortlist does not mean admission is automatic; candidates should follow the instructions provided by the admissions team.

## Scholarships
NST lists several scholarship categories, including:
- Merit-Based Scholarship
- Young Women Leader Scholarship
- Extraordinary Achievement Scholarship
- JEE Excellence Scholarship

Scholarship percentages, eligibility, and what components are covered can vary by campus and admission cycle.

## Help for applicants
If a student asks about current admission dates, eligibility, application status, or documents, NST-Bot should avoid guessing. It should direct the student to the current official NST admissions information or the admissions team.

## Bot rule
Never invent an admission deadline, cutoff, eligibility condition, scholarship amount, or admission decision.
