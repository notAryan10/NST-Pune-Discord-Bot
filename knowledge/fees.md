# NST Fees

## Important
Fees vary by campus, university partner, admission year, scholarship, hostel selection, and other applicable charges.

This file contains current Pune / Ajeenkya DY Patil University information available from the official NST page and should not be treated as a permanent fee schedule.

## Pune program fee
The current official Pune page lists the following tuition and fee components for Semester 1:
- Seat Block Fee: ₹50,000
- Refundable Deposit: ₹50,000
- Upskilling Fee: ₹50,000
- Tuition Fee: ₹1,56,250
- Hostel & Mess: as per selected option
- Listed subtotal before hostel/mess: ₹1,50,000
- Listed tuition: ₹1,56,250
- Total shown before hostel/mess: ₹3,06,250

For Semester 2, the listed components include:
- Upskilling Fee: ₹50,000
- Tuition Fee: ₹1,56,250
- Total shown before hostel/mess: ₹2,06,250

The official fee page should be checked for later semesters and the student's specific admission cycle.

## Other university charges
The Pune information also lists additional charges that may include:
- University registration fee
- Application fee
- Examination fees
- Backlog/re-examination fees
- Summer-term fees
- Convocation/alumni fees
- Other university charges

The exact amount and applicability are controlled by the university and may change.

## Scholarships
NST currently lists scholarship categories such as:
- Merit-Based Scholarship
- Young Women Leader Scholarship
- Extraordinary Achievement Scholarship
- JEE Excellence Scholarship

The scholarship terms determine which fee components are waived.

## Financial aid
NST also lists education-financing partners. Loan interest rates, eligibility, tenure, processing fees, and maximum amounts are lender-dependent and can change.

## Bot behavior
When a student asks:
"What are the fees?"

NST-Bot should first determine the campus if possible.

For Pune:
- State that the figures are from the current official Pune fee information.
- Clearly distinguish tuition, upskilling, seat-block, deposit, hostel/mess, and university charges.
- Warn that fees can change.

Never give a single "4-year total" unless it has been calculated from a verified current fee schedule for the correct campus and admission year.
