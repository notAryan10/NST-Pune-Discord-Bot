# NST Internships

## Overview
NST emphasizes early industry exposure and internships as part of its industry-focused learning model.

The official NST internship information currently reports:
- 93.07% of NST students secured a paid internship by their second year, according to NST's Internship Report 2025.
- Average monthly stipend reported: ₹23,066.
- Top 10% average monthly stipend reported: ₹40,714.
- Highest reported stipend: ₹2.5 lakh/month.

These are reported outcomes and should not be interpreted as a guarantee for an individual student.

## Internship preparation
NST describes its curriculum as preparing students through:
- Programming
- Data Structures and Algorithms
- Web development
- Backend development
- System design
- Data analytics
- Machine learning
- Deep learning
- Projects
- Mentorship
- Interview preparation

## Year-wise exposure
The official curriculum/placement material describes a progression broadly involving:
- Early years: programming, DSA, web development and software foundations
- Middle years: backend development, system design, data analytics and advanced DSA
- Later years: machine learning, deep learning, systems, AI-related subjects and internships

The exact academic structure can change.

## Getting an internship
NST-Bot can help students with:
- Resume preparation
- Project ideas
- DSA practice
- Interview preparation
- Internship search strategy
- Portfolio feedback
- GitHub/project presentation

It should not claim that NST guarantees a particular internship, company, salary, or stipend.

## Company claims
NST's official materials mention students obtaining internships and opportunities with organizations including companies and institutions. Individual examples should be treated as case studies, not guarantees.

## Bot behavior
If a student asks:
"Can I get a Google internship?"

A good answer is:
"NST has reported students receiving Google internships, but the outcome depends on your skills, interview performance, profile, and the specific opportunity. I can help you prepare."

Source: Official Newton School of Technology internship, curriculum, and admissions information, accessed August 2026.
