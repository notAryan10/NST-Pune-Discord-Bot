# NST Hostel

## General
NST describes its undergraduate program as residential. Hostel arrangements and fees depend on the campus/university.

This file is configured primarily for the NST Pune / Ajeenkya DY Patil University context. Do not use these figures for another NST campus.

## Pune hostel options
The official NST Pune information lists multiple hostel options.

### ADYPU in-campus hostel
Current listed cost per semester:
- 2-sharing: ₹1,17,500
- 3-sharing: ₹1,15,000
- 4-sharing: ₹1,10,000

These figures are subject to the official terms and availability.

### NST in-campus Pune options
The current official page also lists:
- Boys, 4-sharing AC: ₹1,20,000 per semester
- Boys, 5-sharing AC: ₹1,17,500 per semester
- Girls, 4-sharing AC: ₹1,20,000 per semester

### Off-campus options
Current listed boys' options include:
- YourSpace 3-sharing AC: ₹1,09,000 per semester
- Tribe 3-sharing AC: ₹1,15,000 per semester
- Tribe 1BHK (2+3 sharing) AC: ₹1,15,000 per semester

Availability and room allocation are subject to the official hostel process.

## Hostel rules
The official Pune information states that hostel accommodation is mandatory for the first two academic years. From the third year onward, independent accommodation may be possible subject to parental consent and university approval.

The official information also states:
- Electricity charges may be additional.
- Hostel fees may be revised annually.
- Hostel allocation is subject to availability.
- Hostel fees generally cover the academic-year accommodation period specified by the university.
- Mess arrangements and transportation depend on the selected hostel option.

## Bot behavior
Hostel prices are time-sensitive. If a student asks for the latest hostel fee, the bot should mention the effective campus and advise checking the latest official fee/hostel notice.

Never assume Pune hostel fees apply to Bengaluru, NCR, Hyderabad, or another campus.

Source: Official Newton School of Technology Ajeenkya DY Patil University Pune fee/hostel information, accessed August 2026.
