# NST Campus

## NST campuses
Newton School of Technology currently lists programs with multiple university partners, including:
- Ajeenkya DY Patil University — Pune
- Rishihood University — Delhi NCR
- S-VYASA University — Bengaluru
- St. Mary's University — Hyderabad

The exact campus a student belongs to matters for university procedures, hostel information, fees, and some administrative processes.

## Pune campus
This knowledge base is primarily intended for the NST Discord server associated with the Pune campus.

Pune is associated with Ajeenkya DY Patil University (ADYPU).

## Residential experience
NST describes the B.Tech program as a 4-year residential program. Students have access to academic learning, project work, mentorship, student clubs, events, and industry-oriented activities.
